Workflow: Bundle to Poster

  1. Export your bundle from the FishView dashboard. The HYBRID layout gives you flat PNGs at images/<Genus_species>.png and per-species replicate folders.
  2. Run recipes — at minimum the three required recipes, plus 1–2 electives.
  3. Generate hero PDFs — each recipe ends with a “hero figure” code chunk that exports a poster-ready PDF at the right dimensions. Run those chunks.
  4. Drop hero PDFs into the template — open the PPTX, delete the placeholder image in each panel, insert your hero PDF (or PNG at 300 dpi).
  5. Fill in the text — title, authors, introduction (3–4 sentences), methods (pipeline summary), conclusions (2–3 bullets), references (3–5), acknowledgments.

Poster Panels

The template has four analysis panels arranged left-to-right across the poster:

Panel What goes here Recipe source Hero PDF name

B — Phylomorphospace PC1 x PC2 biplot with fish images at each species point Phylomorphospace (core recipe) phylomorphospace-PC1-PC2-hero.pdf
C — Phylogeny + Traits Ladderized tree with PC1 ASR branches, tip images, and trait columns (PC1–3 circles + ecology variables) Phylogeny with Traits (core recipe) phylogeny-with-traits-hero.pdf
D — PGLS Results Partial-residual scatter plots testing whether color predicts ecology Rate–Trait Correlation or PGLS Color–Traits recipe pgls-color-traits-hero.pdf

Panel C is the visual hero — give it the most space. Panels A and B sit in the top row; D sits in the right column below B.

Swapping Hero Figures

If your analysis changes or you run additional recipes:

  1. Re-run the hero-PDF chunk in the relevant recipe
  2. In the PPTX, right-click the panel image → Change Picture → select your new PDF/PNG
  3. Resize to fill the panel (maintain aspect ratio)

Each panel in the template is labeled so you know which recipe output goes where.
