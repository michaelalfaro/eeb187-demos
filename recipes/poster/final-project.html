Workflow: Bundle to Poster

  1. Export your bundle from the FishView dashboard. The HYBRID layout gives you flat PNGs at images/<Genus_species>.png and per-species replicate folders.
  2. Run recipes — at minimum the three required recipes, plus 1–2 electives.
  3. Generate hero PDFs — each recipe ends with a “hero figure” code chunk that exports a poster-ready PDF at the right dimensions. Run those chunks.
  4. Drop hero PDFs into the template — open the PPTX, delete the gray placeholder in the Results column, insert your hero PDF (or PNG at 300 dpi).
  5. Fill in all text sections — title, authors, introduction, hypothesis, methods, figure captions, discussion, conclusions, future directions, references, acknowledgments.

Poster Sections

The template follows the standard UCLA scientific poster format with three columns:

Column 1 — Left (Text)

Section What to write Length
INTRODUCTION Background on your clade + why color matters + what’s known 3–4 bullet points
RESEARCH QUESTION Single sentence framing your central question 1–2 sentences
HYPOTHESIS Testable predictions with expected outcomes 2–3 predictions
METHODS pavo pipeline, PCM approach, tree source, R packages 5–6 bullet points

Column 2 — Center (Results / Figures)

This is the visual core of the poster. Drop your hero PDFs here.

Figure Recipe source Hero PDF name
Fig 1: Color Gamut Color Gamut color-gamut-hero.pdf
Fig 2: Phylomorphospace Phylomorphospace (core recipe) phylomorphospace-PC1-PC2-hero.pdf
Fig 3: Phylogeny + Traits Phylogeny with Traits (core recipe) phylogeny-with-traits-hero.pdf
Fig 4: PGLS Rate–Trait Correlation pgls-color-traits-hero.pdf

Each figure needs a caption below it (italic, 20pt) explaining what is shown.

Column 3 — Right (Interpretation)

Section What to write Length
DISCUSSION Interpret results, compare to other clades, explain patterns 3–4 sentences
CONCLUSIONS Key takeaway bullets 2–3 bullets
FUTURE DIRECTIONS Next steps or new questions raised 1–2 sentences
REFERENCES Key citations in APA format 3–5 references

UCLA Brand Colors

The template uses the official UCLA color palette:

Color Hex Used for
UCLA Blue (dark) #003B5C Header bar, footer bar, section headers
UCLA Blue (medium) #2774AE Accents
UCLA Gold #FFD100 Accent stripe, section underlines
White #FFFFFF Body background, header/footer text
Body text #333333 All body text

Use a white background for the poster body. Dark backgrounds reduce readability and waste ink.

Printing

  • Where: Bruin Copy Center or any large-format printer
  • Cost: $51 for up to 42” on one side; $68 for up to 54”
  • Format: Export your finished PPTX as PDF, then print
  • One side must be ≤ 36” (our poster height); the other can be up to ~100” (never relevant)

Swapping Hero Figures

If your analysis changes or you run additional recipes:

  1. Re-run the hero-PDF chunk in the relevant recipe
  2. In the PPTX, right-click the figure image in the Results column → Change Picture → select your new PDF/PNG
  3. Resize to fill the placeholder area (maintain aspect ratio)

Each placeholder in the template is labeled with gray text so you know which figure goes where.
