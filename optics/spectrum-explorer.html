Curves are Planck's law, drawn on the course spectrum axes (300–750 nm, UV shaded, wavelength→color strip under the axis); the inset shows the whole curve so you can see where the peak really is. The atmosphere toggle applies a simplified transmittance template (ozone cut-off, Rayleigh loss, two water/oxygen bands) — it is a teaching template, not a radiative-transfer model. The swatch is an approximate perceived color (CIE 1931 matching functions, analytic fit, sRGB). Cone curves are schematic Gaussians and cone responses are always computed from photon counts.
